# Frozen-versus-refreshed gate: untouched-seed validation protocol v1
Prepared 8 November 2026 after the exposed development comparison. This is a prospective analysis plan for new streams, not a preregistration or retrospective relabeling of development. No validation data have been generated by preparing this package.

## Target claim
Under the specified synthetic generator, three fixed initial training/fitting realizations, reused monitoring calibration and fixed operational settings, does refreshing outcome predictions in the held-out AIPW gate reduce average post-onset treatment-policy loss? The target is a deliberately balanced scenario grid, not an estimated real-world mixture. Do not claim causal-monitoring superiority, new doubly robust methodology, real-world utility, sequential safety or universal dominance.

## Primary endpoint and inference
M1 is the prespecified primary monitoring arm for testing the gate modification. For each fresh stream seed, compute refreshed-minus-frozen mean-per-window post-onset oracle regret in each shifted fit/scenario/onset/cap cell; average those108 equally weighted differences within the seed. Average the100 seed-level values for the primary contrast. Negative favors refreshed. Each onset retains its own available post-onset horizon; normalization does not equalize adaptation opportunities.

Report the primary point estimate, seed-level Monte Carlo SE and a95% paired seed-block percentile-bootstrap interval with5000 resamples and RNG20261108. Resample whole seed-level averages, never dependent periods/methods/caps as separate independent observations. A wholly negative interval supports lower design-average loss in this synthetic target, not universal superiority or safety. No equivalence margin or powered noninferiority/safety claim is specified.100 seeds is a pragmatic Monte Carlo allocation, not a formal power guarantee; disclose attained uncertainty rather than claim adequate power from the three-seed development sample. Do not increase sample size after inspecting significance.

## Secondary and adverse-result reporting
Retain all method/scenario/onset/fit/cap results. Report cumulative loss, mean-window loss, harmful-treatment exposure, reviews and accepted updates. Include stable-window7-12 outcomes as a separate harm diagnostic, not part of the shifted primary average. Compare both variants with treat-all/treat-none/frozen-model benchmarks on the same observations/horizons. Preserve conditional reversals and adverse tails. Secondary cells remain descriptive; do not promote a favorable cell to a new confirmatory primary finding.

Report helpful/harmful/tied accepted-update diagnostics using next-window fixed-model benefit and tolerance1e-10. Final-window benefit is missing, not zero. These events are dependent and finite-sample; do not interpret their count as independent failure probability, sum overlapping diagnostic gains as realizable savings, or infer safety from no observed harmful events. A lower design-average loss does not justify replacing the baseline without considering harms and scope.
